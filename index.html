Profile

SEGUENI Abderraouf

SEGUENI Abderraouf is an Algerian international handball player specialized as a Pivot / Line Player.

A right-handed line player with more than 14 years of competitive handball experience, combining physical power, tactical awareness, positioning and close-range finishing.

His international pathway includes Algeria's U17, U19 and U21 national teams, followed by senior-level international experience.

His profile is built around contact strength, space creation, timing, explosive movement, ball protection and finishing around the 6-metre line.
