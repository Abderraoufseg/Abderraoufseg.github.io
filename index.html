01 — PROFILE

Player
Identity

SEGUENI Abderraouf is an Algerian international handball player specialised as a Pivot / Line Player.

A physically powerful and tactically intelligent player, experienced in 6-metre line play, contact situations, positioning, timing, space creation and close-range finishing.

His international pathway includes Algeria U17, U19 and U21 national teams, African Championship competition and the 2017 U21 World Championship.
