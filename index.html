Player Profile

Elite Handball Profile

SEGUENI Abderraouf is an Algerian international handball player specialised as a Pivot / Line Player.

A right-handed player known for physical strength, tactical intelligence, positioning, timing, space creation and close-range finishing around the 6-metre line.

His international pathway includes Algeria U17, U19 and U21 national teams, African Championship participation and the 2017 U21 World Championship.
