SEGUENI Abderraouf is an Algerian international handball player specialised in the Pivot / Line Player position.

His international pathway includes national-team experience at U17, U19 and U21 level, followed by senior international involvement.

His playing profile combines physical strength, tactical intelligence, space creation and close-range finishing around the six-metre line.
